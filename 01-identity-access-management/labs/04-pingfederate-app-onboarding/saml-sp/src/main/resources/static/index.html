Step 2 · The IdP side

    
Who this app trusts checking…

    
Three facts make an IdP: its entity ID, its SSO URL, and its signing certificate. The certificate is the one that decides whether a login succeeds — it is the public half of the keypair PingFederate signs assertions with. Get it wrong and every assertion comes back invalid_signature, no matter how correct everything else is.

    
loading…


    
Signing certificates we trust

    
Compare the SHA-256 fingerprint with the one in the PingFederate console (SP Connection → Credentials → Digital Signature Settings). Subject names lie — two certificates can share one. A fingerprint is a hash of the whole certificate, so it cannot.
